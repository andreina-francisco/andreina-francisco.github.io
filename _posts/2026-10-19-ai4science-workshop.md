Last week I had the pleasure of hosting our AI for Science workshop, which we organised as a satellite event of this year’s eSSENCE Swedish e-Science Academy. 

I really want to thank to all of our invited speakers, Giovanni Volpe, Niklas Wahlström, Sara Hamis, Maria Bånkestad, Fredrik Lindsten and Gisele Miranda for sharing their insights and expertise, and of course big thanks to my incredible team of co-organizers Anna Persson, Prashant Singh, and Jens Sjölund for making it all happen!

Also special thanks to all of the participants who made it all worth while with their questions and discussions. We really appreciated your time and engagement. 

As a side note, I feel like nowadays everything is a learning experience. This time I learned that I don't know how to take pictures of events. So, for what it's worth, Prashant was not the only person in the audience! I just sat at the very front and was so focused on the talks that I didn't even consider taking photos showcasing all the people. So you'll have to believe me when I say there were more of us there. We also had coffee and excellent cake! I’ll try to have better pictures next time!
